# Lightsaber artwork

`lightsaber.png` is the unmodified static cursor image from Sweezy Cursors'
[Star Wars Obi-Wan Kenobi Lightsaber Animated Cursor](https://sweezy-cursors.com/cursor/star-wars-obi-wan-kenobi-lightsaber-animated/).

Credit: Sweezy Cursors. The source site describes its artwork as original or
transformative fan art for personal, non-commercial use. This asset is not covered
by the repository's source-code license. Obtain appropriate permission before
commercial use or redistribution outside those terms. Star Wars trademarks and
characters belong to their respective owners.

The app displays this static image with CSS colour filters; it does not play the
source cursor's idle animation. The original 128px artwork defines its hotspot at
(20, 12), scaled to (7.5, 4.5) when displayed at 48px.
